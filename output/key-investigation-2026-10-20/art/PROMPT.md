# Prompt da fonte gerada

Ferramenta: `image_gen` integrada, fundo transparente. As imagens da casa adulta e do Fusca foram referências de estilo, sem edição desses arquivos.

```text
Create a production pixel art sprite atlas for the existing Brazilian mystery game O Segredo de Varginha. Use the adult house and original blue VW Beetle reference images visible in this conversation as STYLE REFERENCES ONLY; do not repaint those assets or draw any new characters. Asset sheet: landscape, EXACT 4 columns x 2 rows of equal square cells, transparent background, ample transparent padding in every cell, no grid lines, no letters or labels, no decorative background. Eight distinct isolated assets in reading order: (1) small old brass car ignition key with dark blue round keychain, resting horizontal in overhead 3/4 perspective; (2) SAME key, same size, same colors and identity, angled diagonally for placing down; (3) small torn aged notebook paper scrap with a blue pencil line; (4) closed old red wooden toy chest with navy blue straps and tiny brass latch, matching the toy chest at the foot of the bed in the reference; (5) SAME chest OPEN, key and small blue school notebook visible inside, identical chest proportions and viewpoint; (6) rectangular torn notebook page piece with CHILD'S BLUE PENCIL drawing of a house and low garden wall, aged beige paper; (7) matching torn page piece with a tree, continuing garden wall and branch; (8) matching torn page piece with continuing branch and a tiny FACELESS silhouette. Last three pieces form a coherent left-to-right child's drawing house/tree/silhouette when placed side by side. No words on pages, do not reveal any entity or alien identity. Authentic sharp pixel clusters, dark warm outlines, limited palette, warm cream paper, warm brass, muted navy, blue pencil, upper-left illumination, 3/4 top-down RPG object perspective. The native logical design canvas of EACH cell is 64x64 pixels; render at a uniform integer enlargement with hard pixel boundaries, absolutely NO antialias, blur, gradients, smooth vector lines or soft shadows. Keep object illustrations separate within cell boundaries. Final image must be a clean usable transparent sprite atlas, not a mockup.
```

O gerador retornou 1774×887, sem grid integral e com semitransparência. Por isso, o candidato foi recortado e normalizado como documentado no README; o contrato final é 128×128 por célula, e não o canvas lógico inicialmente solicitado no prompt.
