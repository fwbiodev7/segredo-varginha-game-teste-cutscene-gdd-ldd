## Cabine / shell

Create a NEW pixel-art CABIN SHELL background layer for the blue vintage Fusca, based on the real interior PHOTO. This is a layered game asset: the dashboard will be a SEPARATE sprite, so absolutely DO NOT DRAW ANY DASHBOARD, RADIO, GAUGES, STEERING WHEEL, COLUMN OR POLE in this shell. Also NO DRIVER or hands. Only the cabin shell: blue metal door/window/windshield frame, simple black-vinyl ribbed driver's seat, dark door panel with narrow brown trim, charcoal lower footwell, small mirror, beige roof lining. View tailored to third row of reference2: the original adult driver faces RIGHT in SIDE PROFILE. The original driver occupies x18%-47% of a 16:9 canvas, head y14%-48%, hip near x24% y76%. Black seat back immediately behind his back, modest realistic seat proportions, cushion beneath his hips. Leave the front/right half BELOW the windshield as EMPTY DARK FOOTWELL/BLUE BODY space where a separately positioned dashboard will be composited later. No diagonal empty wall that looks like another seat. Transparent windows and windshield ONLY; all seat/door/floor/metal areas opaque. Use true transparent alpha, no background scenery. Match the PHOTO's blue metal, black seats and short door with black bottom and warm brown top strip. Clean dark pixel outlines and deliberate chunky pixel clusters matching the game's driver sprites, limited-looking shading ramps, no photoreal texture or blur. No labels or UI. Full 16:9 canvas. Remember DASHBOARD MUST BE ABSENT because another game layer supplies it.

## Painel independente

Draw a SEPARATE TRANSPARENT PIXEL-ART DASHBOARD MODULE for the classic blue Fusca in the photo. Only the dashboard assembly floating on transparent background, no car cabin, no seats, no windshield, no person and ABSOLUTELY NO steering wheel, steering shaft/pole or hands. An old thin HORIZONTAL dashboard with warm dark brown woodgrain face, painted blue metal top rim, a small round chrome-rimmed SPEEDOMETER near the LEFT end, period radio immediately to its right, simple small round switches, black glove-compartment panel at RIGHT. Match the photo's shapes and materials, not the previously invented tan bulky dashboard. Mild oblique side perspective suitable behind a driver's RIGHT-FACING profile sprite: left end is the near side, glovebox recedes to the right. Keep overall module slender, roughly 3:1 silhouette, modest rounded left-end lip, dark underside. Left end reaches the existing separate driver's wheel so its connection can stay hidden by that wheel; do NOT add any protruding pole or steering column at all. Speedometer face should be blank near-black inside chrome bezel, no baked needle or lettering, so the game can animate it. Radio face may have simple dark horizontal glass, no bright prelit bars. Crisp deliberate pixel clusters, chunky stepped edges, controlled ramps, no antialias blur or photo texture. Fill most of a wide horizontal canvas with little transparent padding. True alpha transparency around the whole isolated dashboard, no ground shadow or text/UI.
